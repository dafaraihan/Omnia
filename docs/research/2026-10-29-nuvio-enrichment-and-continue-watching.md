## TMDB enrichment

Nuvio's [settings page](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/settings/TmdbSettingsPage.kt#L62) offers a master switch, optional personal key, language, and eleven module switches: trailers, artwork, basic information, details, credits, productions, networks, episodes, season posters, recommendations, and collections. Module controls disable when enrichment is off. [Defaults](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/tmdb/TmdbSettingsRepository.kt#L15) are master off, English, modules on.

The [merge implementation](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/tmdb/TmdbMetadataService.kt#L803) replaces provider artwork, localized title/description, and details with available TMDB values. Existing nonblank IMDb rating wins. Empty TMDB genre/credit lists retain provider values. Episode enrichment matches season/episode, replaces enabled episode fields, and preserves the source release date; season posters have a separate switch. Recommendations replace existing results when TMDB is preferred or existing results are empty. Nonempty TMDB trailers replace source trailers. A [five-second timeout](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/details/MetaDetailsRepository.kt#L274) bounds enrichment and retains original metadata on timeout.

Omnia already has a master switch and nine modules, all defaulting on ([preferences](../../Omnia/Domain/Settings/IntegrationPreferences.swift)), with productions combining studios/networks and episodes combining season data. Its settings explicitly describe gap filling, and its detail flow restricts provider fields by module before merging: [settings](../../Omnia/Features/Settings/MetadataSettingsView.swift), [detail resolution](../../Omnia/Features/Library/MediaDetailView.swift), [merge](../../Omnia/Data/Addons/StremioAddonContent.swift). Existing add-on title, overview, artwork and most populated details win; cast may be replaced when existing credits lack photos. Therefore matching Nuvio requires an explicit precedence decision, not merely matching toggle labels. Omnia's settings text says server-backed only, while its summary also checks personal-key configuration; that copy/configuration consistency needs separate verification before choosing credential UI.

## Continue Watching Card

Nuvio's [Card implementation](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/home/components/HomeContinueWatchingSection.kt#L600) uses a horizontal landscape shelf, aspect ratio 1.77, width equal to poster width × 180/110 × 1.2, configurable corners and three text/padding size tiers. Crop-filled artwork has a lower gradient; bottom-leading episode code, title and episode title remain inside the card. A top-trailing badge reports time left, percent watched when duration is unknown, Up Next, air date, New Episode or New Season. A three-point progress bar sits independently along the bottom edge when progress is positive.

The [artwork policy](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/home/components/HomeContinueWatchingSection.kt#L189) favors episode thumbnails when enabled, otherwise backdrop, then poster and generic image. Upcoming dated episodes favor backdrop. Spoiler blur can obscure unwatched episode artwork. The [settings page](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/settings/ContinueWatchingSettingsPage.kt#L72) previews Card/Wide/Poster and conditionally exposes thumbnail/blur controls. Empty shelves are omitted; absent artwork retains a surface background; controls are disabled during removal animation ([section implementation](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/features/home/components/HomeContinueWatchingSection.kt#L270)). Dedicated card loading/error/retry states were not observed in this component.

Omnia already implements this width formula, landscape overlay, badge, thumbnail fallback, blur, and Card/Wide/Poster selection: [card](../../Omnia/Features/Home/HomeView.swift), [settings](../../Omnia/Features/Settings/ContinueWatchingView.swift). Differences visible in source: fixed text metrics; minimum Card corner radius of 10; black gradient rather than theme-background gradient; progress inside the text stack; badge limited to remaining time, Up Next or year. Omnia additionally offers a locally saved Paused Scene, falling back to artwork, and excludes the previous scene for Next Up. Source does not establish visual parity across Mac, iPad or iPhone; accessibility, resizing, multitasking, orientation, appearance and motion checks remain manual work.

Nuvio [clicks resume and long press opens actions](https://github.com/NuvioMedia/NuvioMobile/blob/53869a0279c59abdef23da2962eb2a1c80e6caa5/composeApp/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/nuvio/app/MainAppContent.kt#L1246); callbacks include restart, manual stream selection and removal. Removing Next Up stores a dismissal; removing a resume item removes progress. Omnia supports configurable direct playback versus details navigation, and native context menus with movie/show navigation and watched-state actions; movie menus expose destructive progress removal, while series menus expose season/episode history operations ([card](../../Omnia/Features/Home/HomeView.swift), [menu commands](../../Omnia/UI/Components/MediaContextMenu.swift)). Preserve intentional Omnia actions when adapting appearance. Native context menus and accessible keyboard actions should remain usable across Mac, pointer/keyboard iPad and touch iPhone.
